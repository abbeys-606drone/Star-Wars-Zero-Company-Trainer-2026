## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

During an operator's turn, movement consumes action points and the tactical map shows reachable locations and their costs. Use the unlimited action points option before moving to freely reposition operators, take cover, and prepare attacks or overwatch without ending the turn.

### Tools & Inventory

The Den allows players to purchase equipment and customize operator loadouts, appearances, specializations, and talents. Activate unlimited credits or free equipment upgrades before buying gear, then disable the option after finishing the loadout to reduce unnecessary changes to the save.

### World & Environment

The Den serves as the company's base of operations, where players select missions, manage operators, upgrade facilities, and prepare equipment. Intel is used for non-combat operations, while credits and influence support strategic progression. Resource options are most useful from the base or galaxy-map screens.

### Combat & Weapons

Each operator normally receives three action points for movement, attacks, abilities, rally actions, and overwatch. Advantage points are earned by damaging enemies and are spent on powerful special actions. Enable unlimited health, action points, Advantage, or accuracy before issuing commands, and disable mission-completion features before scripted objectives or story transitions.
